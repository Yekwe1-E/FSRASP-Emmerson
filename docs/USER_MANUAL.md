# FSARAP System User Manual
## Faculty of Science Academic Repository and Assessment Portal
### Niger Delta University (NDU)

---

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the official User Manual for the **Faculty of Science Academic Repository and Assessment Portal (FSARAP)**. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for Students, Lecturers, Faculty Admins, and Super Administrators.

---

## 2. Student User Guide

### 2.1 Registration & Login
1. Open the portal home page (`index.html`).
2. Click **"Register / Login"** in the top navigation bar.
3. Select **"Student Account"**, enter your details, select your Department and Level (e.g. Computer Science, 300 Level), and click **Register Account**.
4. Log in with your email and password.

### 2.2 Browsing & Downloading Lecture Materials
1. Click **"Repository"** in the top navigation bar.
2. Filter materials using the **Academic Level Tabs (100L - 500L)** or select your **Department** from the filter sidebar.
3. Type keywords in the search bar (e.g., "CSC 311", "Microbiology").
4. Click on any material card to view full details.
5. Click **"Download File"** to download the lecture note directly to your device.
6. Click the star icon (⭐) to bookmark the material for quick access from your Student Dashboard.

### 2.3 Taking Self-Assessment Quizzes
1. Click **"Quizzes"** in the navigation bar.
2. Select an active quiz for your course and click **"Take Quiz"**.
3. Review the instructions and duration, then start the test.
4. Select your answers using the radio buttons and click **"Next"** to move between questions.
5. Watch the **Time Remaining** countdown timer at the top right.
6. When finished, click **"Submit Assessment Now"** to view your instant percentage score, pass/fail result, and detailed question explanations.

## 3. Lecturer User Guide

### 3.1 Uploading Lecture Materials
1. Log in to your Lecturer account.
2. Click **"Upload Material"** from the navigation header or Lecturer Dashboard.
3. Fill in the Material Title, select Department, Academic Level, Semester, Session, and Course Code.
4. Choose the category (Lecture Notes, Practical Manual, Past Questions, etc.).
5. Attach your file (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, or ZIP up to 100MB) and click **"Upload Material"**.
6. Your material will be uploaded directly to Supabase Storage and made accessible to students.

### 3.2 Creating Self-Assessment Quizzes
1. Click **"Create New Quiz"** from the Quizzes page or Lecturer Dashboard.
2. Enter the Quiz Title, Course, Duration (in minutes), Pass Mark (%), and Max Allowed Attempts.
3. Click **"Create Quiz Header"**.
4. Type your questions, enter Option A, B, C, D, select the correct option radio button, and click **"Save Question to Quiz"**.

---

## 4. Super Administrator Guide

### 4.1 Approving New Lecturers
1. Log in with Super Admin credentials (`admin@ndu.edu.ng`).
2. Navigate to **"Admin Dashboard"**.
3. In the **"Pending Lecturer Approvals"** table, review staff ID details and click **"Approve"** to grant upload privileges.

### 4.2 System Audit & User Account Management
1. View overall platform analytics (Total Students, Lecturers, Materials, Downloads).
2. Toggle account activation/deactivation for any user in the **User Accounts Management** table.
3. Review live operational logs in the **Recent Audit Logs** panel.
